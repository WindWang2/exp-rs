## Round 2 — independent review findings and fixes

Two adversarial reviewers (ownership/lifetime; spec/traceability) worked from the
uncommitted worktree. Findings:

1. **P3 (real, introduced by the temp branch): re-entrancy/aliasing in the

   and only nulled `mTempConnection` *after* `emit connectionCreated` /
   `notifyWorkflowChanged`. A synchronous listener mutating the scene would
   either hit `removeConnection(temp)` (deletes item, leaves a dangling set
   entry) or `cancelTempConnection()` (double delete). Fixed in

   aliases **before** `setTargetPort`/insert/emits.
